# Aldermoor Retail: Sales Analysis and Dashboard (Excel)

## Overview
An Excel analysis of 15,818 order lines (£970k net sales, January 2023 to December 2025) for Aldermoor Retail, a fictional 10-store UK retailer with an online shop. The project takes a messy sales export through cleaning and analysis to an interactive sales dashboard.

## Business questions
1. Are sales growing year on year?
2. Which channels, stores and products drive sales?
3. Who are the most valuable customers?

## What I did
- **Cleaning:** fixed 12 kinds of data-quality issue in the Sales sheet and 8 in the Customers sheet, then reconciled to control totals (15,818 lines, £970,369.89 net sales, 420 customers)
- **Sort and filter:** organised the data to check and explore it
- **Formulas:** aggregation functions such as SUMIFS, COUNTIFS and AVERAGEIFS
- **Lookups:** VLOOKUP, HLOOKUP and XLOOKUP to bring product, store and customer details into the sales data
- **PivotTables and PivotCharts:** sales by year, month, channel, store, category and product
- **Dashboard:** KPI cards, PivotCharts and slicers on one interactive page

## Key findings
- Sales grew 11.4% in 2025 to £355k
- Online grew 22% against 6.4% for stores, reaching 35% of sales
- The top 5 products make 29.0% of sales
- Trade accounts are 10% of customers but 24.5% of sales
